Growly Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

Growly is a personal growth app. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: your entries stay on your phone, and we do not run a server that receives them.

What stays on your device

Everything you record in Growly, such as your entries, sleep, time and skill topics, is stored only on your device in an encrypted database. We do not upload it, we cannot see it, and we do not sell or share it.

Sign-in

You can sign in with an email address and password, or with your Google account. Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication, a Google service. Firebase receives your email address (and, for Google sign-in, basic profile details) to create and verify your account. See Firebase privacy information.

Subscriptions and payments

Growly offers an optional paid subscription through Google Play. Google Play handles all payments. Growly never sees or stores your card or bank details. The app keeps a record on your device of which plan you have and when it renews, so it can unlock features offline.

Permissions

  • Notifications: used for reminders you choose to turn on.
  • Biometrics / device lock: used only if you turn on the app lock, to confirm it is you. Your fingerprint or face data never leaves the device's secure system.

Analytics and advertising

Growly does not show ads and does not use advertising identifiers or third-party tracking.

Children

Growly is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes all data stored on your device. To delete your sign-in account, email us at the address below and we will remove it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date.
